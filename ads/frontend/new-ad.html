What works on Meta
  • Vertical media (4:5 for feeds, 9:16 for Stories and Reels). Keep text away from the top and bottom 14%.
  • Hook in the first second; most people watch muted, so put the words on screen.
  • The first ~125 characters of the primary text show before "See more": lead with the problem.
  • Headlines read best under 40 characters.
  • A genuinely different idea beats a small variation of one that's running.
